# **App Name**: Foodie Kingdom

## Core Features:

- Menu Display with Photos and MRP: Showcase restaurant menus with high-quality photos and clearly listed prices (MRP) for each item.
- Free Delivery Zone Indicator: Automatically detect the user's location and highlight restaurants within a 1 km radius that offer free delivery.
- Smart Search and Filters: Enable users to quickly find food by cuisine, dish name, or vendor using smart search and filter options.
- Real-Time Order Tracking: Provide live updates and tracking of the delivery executive's location from the restaurant to the user's doorstep.
- Local Restaurant Promotion: Prioritize and promote local restaurants and vendors, highlighting authentic regional flavors alongside international cuisines.
- Multiple Payment Options: Support secure payment methods including digital wallets, card payments, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
- AI Recommendation Tool: AI-powered recommendation tool to suggest dishes or restaurants based on user's order history, preferences, and real-time factors such as popular local choices and time of day.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vibrant orange (#FF7800) to evoke appetite and energy.
- Background color: Light tan (#F2EBCD), creating a warm and inviting feel.
- Accent color: Mustard yellow (#FFDA63), complementing the orange for highlights and CTAs.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans', a versatile sans-serif offering a balance of modernity and approachability.
- Use clear and intuitive icons to represent different cuisines and menu categories.
- Design a clean and easy-to-navigate layout, ensuring seamless browsing and ordering experience.
- Implement subtle animations to indicate loading, add items to the cart, and confirm orders.
